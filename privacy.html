Privacy notice

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Ticketly helps you discover events and organize details for tickets you have purchased elsewhere. It does not sell or issue event tickets.

Information handled

Supabase Auth processes your email address, password credential, and account/session data so you can sign in and recover your account. Ticketly does not receive or store your plaintext password.

If you add a ticket, Ticketly stores the event name, venue, optional date, ticket quantity, optional barcode/ticket code, and an optional image or PDF attachment in your account's Supabase database/storage. Barcode values and ticket files are sensitive: only add information you need.

The site also requests live event listing information from the configured discovery provider. The API server may process request metadata needed to return those listings. Hosting providers may maintain operational access logs.

How your account data is protected

Ticket records are associated with your Supabase user ID and protected by row-level security. Ticket attachments are stored in a private bucket and opened using a short-lived signed link. Never publish a Supabase service-role key. These protections depend on correctly applying the supplied SQL and keeping project credentials secure.

Ticket validity

Details and barcode data you save are for your personal organization only. Ticketly does not verify a purchase, validate a barcode, provide an admission credential, or guarantee acceptance at a venue. Use the original ticket provider for entry and ticket support.

Retention and your choices

You can edit or remove saved ticket details and attached files through your account. Deleting your Supabase account should cascade-delete ticket rows; confirm your project’s account-deletion process and storage cleanup before public launch. You may also contact the site operator to request account assistance. Do not use this sample notice as a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal review.

Service providers and changes

Supabase provides authentication, database, and file storage; Render/Vercel may host the API or website; an event-discovery provider supplies listing links. Configure provider retention and processing terms for your deployment. This notice should be reviewed and customized with your business contact and applicable legal requirements before launch.
